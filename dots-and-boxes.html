How to play
  • Pick a board (Small, Medium or Large) and type how much you want to play for.
  • A coin flip decides who goes first.
  • Take turns drawing one line between two dots next to each other.
  • Draw the 4th side of a box and it’s yours, and you go again.
  • Don’t hand her a box: drawing the 3rd side lets the next player take it.
  • When every line is drawn, whoever has more boxes wins. Only Medium can end in a tie.
