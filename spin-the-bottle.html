How to play
  • Pick a wedge. The amounts are hidden and shuffled every spin, so every wedge is a blind pick.
  • Lock in & Spin. All the amounts are revealed and the bottle spins. Once it’s spinning, there’s no backing out.
  • The bottle lands on another wedge: you lose that wedge’s amount.
  • The bottle lands on YOUR wedge: you lose double your wedge.
  • Every wedge is equally likely (1 in 8). Your wallet needs to cover the worst case: double the biggest wedge.
