5 in a Row

18+ GAMES

You MUST be 18 or older
to participate in this feature.
21+ preferred.

How to play
  • Tap a column to drop your piece. It falls to the lowest open spot.
  • You and Gnomie take turns. Your pieces are your color, hers are pink.
  • First to line up 5 wins: across, up and down, or diagonal.
  • Block her lines before she gets to 5. If the board fills up with no winner, it’s a tie.
