Dice Duel

18+ GAMES

You MUST be 18 or older
to participate in this feature.
21+ preferred.

How to play
  • Take turns. Tap Roll, then tap one of your 3 columns to put the die there. Gnomie does the same on her side.
  • Pairs and triples turn pink. Two of the same number in a column count double. Three count triple, so three 4s = 36.
  • Knock out her dice. Your die removes any of Gnomie’s matching numbers in the column straight across. She can do it to you too.
  • The game ends when either board is full. Higher total wins.
