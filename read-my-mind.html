Read My Mind

18+ GAMES

You MUST be 18 or older
to participate in this feature.
21+ preferred.

How to play
  • Gnomie is thinking of a 6-letter word. You get 6 tries to guess it.
  • Before each guess, pick how much that guess is worth. Pennies are fine. Every guess takes its amount, right or wrong.
  • After each guess the tiles show: your color = right letter, right spot; gold = in the word, wrong spot; dark = not in the word.
  • Guess it and you’ve read her mind. Miss all 6 and she tells you the word.
  • A new word every game. You won’t get the same word twice until you’ve seen them all.
