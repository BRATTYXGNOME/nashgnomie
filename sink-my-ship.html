Sink My Ship

18+ GAMES

You MUST be 18 or older
to participate in this feature.
21+ preferred.

How to play
  • Place your fleet: ships of 4, 3, 3 and 2 squares on your 8×8 sea. Drag them, or tap a ship and then tap where it goes. Rotate turns it.
  • Type how much you want to play for, then press Ready.
  • Take turns firing at one square of Gnomie’s sea. You go first.
  • A hit means you fire again. The same goes for her.
  • First to sink the whole other fleet wins. There are no ties.
