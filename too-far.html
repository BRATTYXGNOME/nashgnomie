Don’t Bust (31)

18+ GAMES

You MUST be 18 or older
to participate in this feature.
21+ preferred.

How to play
  • Get as close to 31 as you can without going over. (31, because Gnomie’s birthday is August 31st.) Cards are numbered 1 to 11.
  • Type how much you want to play for. The boxes show what you lose if you win, tie, lose or bust.
  • You take turns. On your turn, Draw another card or Stay. Then Gnomie takes her turn by her own rule.
  • Once you stay, Gnomie keeps playing until she stops.
  • Closer to 31 wins. If Gnomie goes over 31, you win.
  • Go over 31 yourself and you bust: you lose the most.
