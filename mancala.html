Mancala

18+ GAMES

You MUST be 18 or older
to participate in this feature.
21+ preferred.

How to play
  • Pick a pit on your side (the bottom row). All its beads are scooped up and dropped one at a time into the next pits, going right and around the board.
  • Your store is on the right. Beads you drop there are yours to keep. You skip Gnomie’s store.
  • Last bead lands in your store? You get another turn.
  • Last bead lands in an empty pit on your side? You capture it plus every bead in Gnomie’s pit straight across.
  • When one side runs out, each player keeps what’s left on their own side. Most beads in your store wins.
