18+ GAMES

You MUST be 18 or older
to participate in this feature.
21+ preferred.

How to play
  • Type how much each wrong letter costs. Pennies are fine. The most a round can cost (6 misses) is set aside, and whatever you don’t use comes back.
  • Gnomie picks a word and shows you a hint, like “Gear” or “Findom”.
  • Guess one letter at a time. Right letters are free and fill in the blanks.
  • Each wrong letter costs your amount, and Gnomie puts a piece of clothing back on. She starts in her lingerie… six misses and she’s fully dressed.
  • Spell the word before 6 misses. You won’t get the same word twice until you’ve had them all.
